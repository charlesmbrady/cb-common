Introduction

A fleet of AI cars spawns with a simple brain. They start clumsy, but you can improve them by saving good runs or letting the trainer loop pick the best and mutate it.

Manual training

Watch or drive, then hit 💾 when the focused car looks best (usually the farthest or most cars passed). Restart to spawn variants using the current mutation rate.

Automated training

Press 🏋️ to auto-train. After each window (default 10s, configurable) the best car by cars-passed and distance is kept, mutated, and used to spawn the next generation. Adjust iterations, window, and mutation in settings; press 🏋️ again to stop.

How to drive

  • Arrow keys: steer when in KEYS mode.
  • Space or 🔑/🤖: toggle manual vs AI driving.
  • ⏸️/▶️: pause or resume the simulation.
  • ♻️: restart the run (applies saved settings).
  • 🏋️: start automated training cycles.
  • 💾 save the best brain; 🗑️ clear saved brain.
  • ℹ️ reopen these instructions.
  • ⚙️ settings (cars, follow ratio, network view) apply on restart.

Settings






Changes apply the next time you restart (♻️).
